About

I'm Aarit Dixit, and I build things that fly. R.A.M.P.A.T. began in 2024 as a foam-and-cardboard prototype and grew into a two-year project that pulled me into CAD, electronics, aerodynamics and control software.

What ties my projects together is access: capable aircraft and instruments built for a fraction of what they usually cost, with every file shared so others can build on them.
